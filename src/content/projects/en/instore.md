## Context

Mercado Pago is Mercado Libre's fintech and one of the largest digital banks in Latin America. **InStore** is the in-store payments area, connecting buyers and sellers at checkout in physical stores.

## What I do

- Building InStore apps and **mobile libraries** consumed by the Mercado Pago app
- **QR code payment** flows: generation and scanning
- **Point-of-sale checkout** and card terminal integration
- Transaction processing in physical retail environments
